# Amity University Gurugram — Student FAQ

## Attendance Policy
- Minimum attendance required to be eligible for end-semester exams: **75%**.
- Students with a valid medical certificate or approved exemption
  (B-category condonation) may be allowed to sit for exams with a
  minimum of **70% attendance**, subject to approval.
- Attendance shortage beyond the condonation limit results in being
  **detained** from appearing in that semester's exams.
- Attendance is calculated per subject — check subject-wise attendance
  regularly on the Amizone portal.

## Exam Rules
1. **Attempts:** Students get **one attempt per subject per year**.
2. **Revaluation:** Students can apply to have their answer sheets
   re-evaluated for a fee of **₹500** per subject, within the window
   announced after results are declared.
3. **Backlog rules:** If a student has a backlog (whether due to
   failing or being debarred/detained for attendance shortage), it
   must be cleared **the following year**, with a backlog clearance
   fee of **₹1000** per subject.
4. **Malpractice/UFM (Unfair Means):** Use of unfair means during
   exams leads to disciplinary action as per university policy.
5. **Hall ticket/admit card:** Issued only once attendance and fee
   dues are cleared.

## Fee Payment
- Fees are payable through the official **Amizone** student portal
  (amizone.net).
- Late fee payment beyond the due date attracts a penalty as notified
  each semester on the portal/notice board.
- Fee receipts and dues can be checked anytime under the "Fee" section
  of the portal.
- Backlog clearance fee: ₹1000 per subject (see Exam Rules above).
- Revaluation fee: ₹500 per subject (see Exam Rules above).

## Portal (Amizone) FAQs
- **Login issues:** Use your enrollment number and registered password;
  use "Forgot Password" on the login page to reset, or contact the
  admission office if the account is locked.
- **Timetable:** Available under the "Timetable" section after logging
  into Amizone.
- **Results:** Published under the "Examination" section once declared;
  contact the Examination Department if a result seems incorrect or
  is missing.
- **Fee receipts/penalty details:** Available under the "Fee" section
  of the portal.

## Academic & Campus Life FAQs

- **How do I get my ID card reissued if lost?**
  Report the loss to the Admission Office (Block A) with a written
  application; a duplicate ID card is issued for a nominal fee.

- **What is the grading system?**
  Amity follows a relative grading system based on CGPA, evaluated per
  semester based on internal assessments and end-semester exams.

- **Can I get a transcript or bonafide certificate?**
  Yes, apply through the Amizone portal under "Certificate Requests"
  or directly at the Admission Office (Block A); processing usually
  takes a few working days.

- **What is the process for hostel accommodation?**
  Contact the Hostel/Accommodation Office separately from academic
  admissions; allotment is typically done at the start of each
  academic year based on availability.

- **How do I join a club or society?**
  Clubs and societies open registrations at the start of each semester;
  check notices on Amizone or the notice boards for sign-up links.

- **What happens if I miss an internal assessment/quiz?**
  Contact your subject faculty directly with a valid reason (medical
  certificate, etc.); make-up assessments are granted at the faculty's
  discretion, not automatically.

- **Where do I raise a complaint about faculty or academics?**
  Complaints can be raised through the Department Office or the
  Dean's office, depending on severity.
